03 — Profile

HAYATO
TAKAHASHI

Photographer / Visual Creator

Photographer and visual creator based in Tokyo and Kanagawa. After graduating from the Department of Photography at Nihon University College of Art, focuses on portrait, fashion, art, and advertising — handling personal photography and brand visual production.

With an intuitive grasp of facial expressions and spatial texture, specialises in visual work combining photography, video, and direction. Works span promotional shots, profile photos, artist portraits, fashion shoots, live photography, store and brand visuals, and event coverage for both individuals and companies.

Also active as an artist through exhibitions and photobook production, pursuing a balance between commercially refined imagery and lasting artistic expression. Currently operating as "TAKAHASHI LAB," with photography and video at the core of all creative work.
